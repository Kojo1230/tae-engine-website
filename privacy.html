Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

TAE Engine is under development. This policy must be updated if the actual application, its data handling or its service providers change before launch.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you choose and authorize, TAE Engine may process account profile information, video information and access tokens provided through approved platform authorization. The application should request only the information needed for its enabled features.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide authorized account connections, display supported profile and video information, operate requested content workflows, troubleshoot errors and protect the service.

3. Authorization and third parties

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok's authorization process and permissions determine what information may be accessed. TikTok processes information under its own policies. Please review the applicable TikTok privacy notices as well.

4. Storage and security

Any live version of TAE Engine must document its actual storage, retention and security practices. Access tokens and other sensitive information must be protected from unauthorized access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Personal information should not be sold. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the service using disclosed providers, comply with applicable law, protect rights or respond to security incidents. Actual providers and disclosures must be documented before launch.

6. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the disclosed purpose or legal requirements. Before launch, TAE Engine must provide a working process for users to request deletion of applicable personal information and revoke account access.

7. Your choices

You can choose whether to authorize account access and should be able to revoke permissions through the applicable platform settings. Additional privacy rights may apply depending on your location.

8. Children

TAE Engine is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service develops. The date at the top indicates when this page was last reviewed.
